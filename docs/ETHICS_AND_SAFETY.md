# Ethics, Safety and Malware Non-Distribution Statement

This project concerns ransomware behavior and therefore requires explicit
safety boundaries.

## No raw malware distribution

The public research release must not include raw ransomware executables,
malware archives or other live malware payloads.

Cryptographic hashes and metadata are provided for identity verification.

## Analysis reproduction

The primary reproducibility path uses the retained telemetry/evidence dataset
and does not require malware acquisition or execution.

## Controlled dataset reconstruction

Some source scripts document the original sample-selection or
materialization workflow. Their publication serves methodological
transparency and does not constitute authorization to perform malware
handling.

Any researcher attempting such work is independently responsible for:

- legal authorization;
- institutional policy compliance;
- ethics approval where applicable;
- secure malware-laboratory controls;
- network and VM isolation;
- source-service terms;
- credential security;
- safe storage, transfer and destruction procedures.

## Responsibility

The materials are provided for legitimate research and reproducibility.
Researchers remain responsible for their own use, operational decisions,
security controls and compliance obligations.

A disclaimer does not replace applicable law, institutional requirements,
or responsible malware-handling practice.
